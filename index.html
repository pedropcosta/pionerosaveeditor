New employees join without tasks, like a fresh hire from the agency. They appear when the next working day starts; assign them their tasks in the game. Names are generated automatically and can be edited before saving.

Vehicles

RepairRefuelVehiclePlateOwnerFuelEngine damageBody

Engine damage builds up with collisions; when it is high the engine light comes on, the car smokes and loses speed. Body damage is the dents in the bodywork. Repairing a vehicle fixes both. Refuelling fills the tank to its full capacity; it is only available for models whose tank size is confirmed.

New vehicles

VehicleColourOwnerPlate

New vehicles are delivered like new ones from the dealer: full tank, no damage, empty cargo. They appear in a row just in front of where your character was standing when the game was saved, so save while standing outside in an open area. Kabestie, Cangu and Cangu Deluxe can only be company vehicles, Classic Endurance and Secuestrator can be either, and all other models are personal only. Drive company vehicles into a warehouse yourself; parking spots (Iron Yard 6, Grand Depot 8 to 14, Cargo Garage 1 to 2) are assigned as they become free, and extra vehicles wait their turn.
