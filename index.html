Vehicles

Engine damage builds up with collisions; when it is high the engine light comes on, the car smokes and loses speed. Body damage is the dents in the bodywork. Repairing a vehicle fixes both. Refuelling fills the tank to its full capacity; it is only available for models whose tank size is confirmed. Cargo slots can be raised up to 48 (six rows of 8 in the inventory screen); slots holding items cannot be removed. Changing a vehicle's model keeps its plate, colour, owner, driver and cargo, sets the cargo slots to the new model's size (never fewer than the slots in use), clears the dents and caps the fuel to the new tank.

New vehicles

VehicleColourOwnerPlate

New vehicles are delivered like new ones from the dealer: full tank, no damage, empty cargo. They appear in a row just in front of where your character was standing when the game was saved, so save while standing outside in an open area. Kabestie, Cangu and Cangu Deluxe can only be company vehicles, Classic Endurance and Secuestrator can be either, and all other models are personal only. Drive company vehicles into a warehouse yourself; parking spots (Iron Yard 6, Grand Depot 8 to 14, Cargo Garage 1 to 2) are assigned as they become free, and extra vehicles wait their turn.

Storage

Stock of every item on the storage shelves of each property, against the capacity of the shelves assigned to it (shelves × 40 slots × stack size). Refill fills every slot of the assigned shelves with that item.

Stack sizes: consumables 12, products 6, packed items 1. Refilled slots copy their hidden data from an existing item of the same kind in your save, so an item the save has never contained cannot be refilled. Shelves holding other items are left alone.
